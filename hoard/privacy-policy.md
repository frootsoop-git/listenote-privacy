# Privacy Policy for Hoard

*Last updated: November 20, 2026*

## Overview

Hoard ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information Hoard uses, where it goes, and how it is protected when you use the Hoard iOS app.

**The short version: your photos and screenshots never leave your phone.**

## Information We Collect

### Photos and Screenshots

Hoard asks for access to your photo library so it can turn your screenshots and photos into mosaic artwork. All of this happens on your device. Hoard reads small thumbnails and colors from your photos, stores them in a cache on your phone, and builds your artwork on your phone. Your photos, screenshots, thumbnails, and finished artwork are never uploaded to our servers or to any third party.

### Library Stats

Stats such as your screenshot count, busiest day, and camera-roll facts are calculated on your device from photo dates and labels. They are not sent to us.

### Something Wild Ideas

When you use the "Something wild" feature, Hoard sends the following to our server: the idea you type (or a "Surprise me" request), up to five color codes from your library's most common colors, and the titles of your recent Hoard pieces so new ideas don't repeat. No photos are sent. Our server passes this text to OpenAI to create a picture, which is sent back to your phone and rebuilt there from your own photos.

### Device Identifier

Hoard creates a random identifier on your phone. It is not linked to your name, email, Apple ID, or any other personal information. We use it only to count your Something wild tries and to link your purchases to your phone.

### Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. When you buy Hoard Pro or more tries, we receive purchase information from Apple (transaction ID, product purchased, price, and currency) so we can confirm the purchase and add your tries.

We do not use accounts. We do not collect your name, email address, location, or contacts.

## How We Use Your Information

- Your Something wild idea, color codes, and recent titles are used only to create your picture. They are sent to OpenAI's GPT-4o mini to write an image description, and to OpenAI's image model to create the picture.
- Your device identifier and purchase information are used only to count tries, confirm purchases, and restore Hoard Pro.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising. Hoard contains no ads and no tracking.

## Third-Party Services

Hoard uses the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy:

| Service | Purpose | Privacy Policy |
|---|---|---|
| OpenAI | Creating Something wild pictures from your ideas | [openai.com/privacy](https://openai.com/privacy) |
| Supabase | Our server: counting tries and confirming purchases | [supabase.com/privacy](https://supabase.com/privacy) |
| Apple | App Store purchases | [apple.com/legal/privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) |

According to OpenAI's API policies, data sent through its API is not used to train its models by default and may be kept for a limited time for abuse monitoring.

## Sharing

Hoard only shares your artwork or stats when you tap Share, using your iPhone's share sheet. You choose where it goes.

## Data Storage and Security

- Your device identifier, try counts, Hoard Pro status, and purchase records are stored on Supabase servers located in the United States.
- Your ideas and generated pictures are not stored on our servers.
- All data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections.

## Data Retention

- Photo thumbnails, colors, and your recent artwork stay on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them.
- Try counts and purchase records are kept so your tries and purchases keep working and can be restored. They contain no personal information.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Control photo access:** change or remove Hoard's photo access at any time in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos > Hoard.
- **Delete on-device data:** delete the Hoard app.
- **Request deletion of server records:** contact us at the email below, and we will delete records associated with your device within 30 days.

## Children's Privacy

Hoard is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
